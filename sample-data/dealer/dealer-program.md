# Ridgeline Dealer Program — Terms & Pricing (DEALERS ONLY)

## Pricing tiers

Pricing is based on units ordered in the trailing 12 months.

| Tier | Annual units | Price per Clear panel | Price per Smoke Tint panel |
|------|--------------|-----------------------|----------------------------|
| Bronze | 1–24 | $219 | $249 |
| Silver | 25–99 | $199 | $229 |
| Gold | 100+ | $179 | $205 |

Suggested retail price is $329 (Clear) and $369 (Smoke Tint). Dealers may not advertise below $299.

## Ordering process

1. Email your purchase order to the dealer desk with SKUs and quantities.
2. You'll receive an order confirmation and invoice within 1 business day.
3. Payment terms are Net 30 for Silver and Gold dealers; Bronze dealers pay by card at order.
4. Orders of 10 or more panels ship by freight on a pallet at no charge.

## Program terms

- Dealers must maintain a physical showroom or service location.
- Display units are available at 50% off the Bronze price, limited to two per location per year.
- Either party may end the program with 30 days' written notice.
