# Ridgeline Dealer Program — Terms (DEALERS ONLY)

## Pricing

Dealer pricing is not published. Dealers receive pricing directly from our team by email.

## Ordering process

1. Email your purchase order to the dealer desk with SKUs and quantities.
2. You'll receive an order confirmation and invoice within 1 business day.
3. Orders of 10 or more panels ship by freight on a pallet at no charge.

## Program terms

- Dealers must maintain a physical showroom or service location.
- Display units are available, limited to two per location per year.
- Either party may end the program with 30 days' written notice.
