# Care & Cleaning

## Everyday cleaning

Rinse the windshield with plain water first to float away grit. Then wash with a mild dish soap mixed in lukewarm water, using a soft microfiber cloth. Rinse again and pat dry with a clean microfiber towel.

## What NOT to use

- Paper towels or shop rags (they leave fine scratches)
- Ammonia-based glass cleaners such as standard window sprays
- Alcohol, acetone, gasoline, or any solvent
- Abrasive pads, squeegees, or pressure washers held closer than 3 feet

Using these products can cloud or craze the polycarbonate and is not covered by the warranty.

## Removing light scratches

Light surface scuffs can be reduced with a plastic polish made for polycarbonate. Work in small circles with a microfiber pad. Deep scratches cannot be polished out.

## Storage

If you remove the windshield for the off-season, store it flat or upright on its edge, out of direct sunlight, with the protective film or a soft blanket over it.
