# Warranty & Returns Policy

## Limited warranty

Ridgeline Trail Series windshields carry a 3-year limited warranty against yellowing, delamination of the hard coat, and bracket failure under normal use. The warranty starts on the delivery date and covers the original purchaser only.

The warranty does not cover damage from impacts, accidents, improper cleaning products, modifications, or commercial fleet use. Fleet customers should ask about our separate fleet warranty.

## Making a warranty claim

Email photos of the issue and your order number to our support team. We'll reply within 2 business days. Approved claims receive a replacement panel or bracket at no cost, including shipping.

## Returns

Unused windshields in their original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the product price. The protective film must still be on both sides.

Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or we shipped the wrong part. A 15% restocking fee applies to returns of Smoke Tint panels, because they are cut to order.

## Damaged in shipping

Inspect your windshield when it arrives. If it's damaged, report it within 7 days with photos of the box and the panel, and we'll ship a replacement at no charge.
