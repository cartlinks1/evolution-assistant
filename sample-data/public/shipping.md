# Shipping Policy

## Where we ship

We ship to the contiguous 48 United States. We do not currently ship to Alaska, Hawaii, U.S. territories, or outside the U.S.

## Cost

Standard ground shipping is free on all windshield orders. Expedited 2-day shipping is available for $49.

## Timing

Clear (CLR) windshields ship within 2 business days of your order. Smoke Tint (TNT) windshields are cut to order and ship within 7 business days. Ground delivery then takes 3–6 business days depending on your location.

## Tracking

You'll receive a tracking number by email as soon as your order leaves our warehouse.
