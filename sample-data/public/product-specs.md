# Ridgeline Trail Series Windshield — Product Specifications

## Material

Every Ridgeline Trail Series windshield is cut from 3/16" (4.8 mm) AS-4 rated polycarbonate sheet with a hard-coated, scratch-resistant exterior surface.

Certification: The Trail Series polycarbonate is certified AS-4 under ANSI/SAE Z26.1-1996, tested by Northfield Test Labs (report NTL-2291, issued March 2025). Each panel carries a permanent AS-4 mark in the lower passenger-side corner.

UV protection: Blocks 99% of UVA and UVB light, per ASTM E903 testing by Northfield Test Labs (report NTL-2304).

## Tint options

- **Clear (CLR)** — maximum visibility, recommended for evening and early-morning play.
- **Smoke Tint (TNT)** — a light gray tint that reduces glare. Not available for every cart; see the fitment list.

## Dimensions

Dimensions vary by cart. Typical Trail Series panels measure 44"–49" wide and 32"–36" tall. The exact size for your cart is listed on its product page.

## What's in the box

- One Trail Series windshield panel with protective film on both sides
- Two powder-coated aluminum mounting brackets
- Stainless steel hardware kit (4 bolts, 4 nylon lock nuts, 8 washers)
- Four rubber isolator pads
- Printed installation guide

No drilling is required for any cart on our fitment list.

## Fold-down design

Trail Series windshields fold down in one motion for airflow on hot days. Two thumb-knob latches hold the panel upright; loosen both and tilt the top of the panel forward to fold it.
